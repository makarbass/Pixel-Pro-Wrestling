# Pixel Pro Wrestling

**Version 0.0.1**

Pixel Pro Wrestling is a single-player browser wrestling game with pixel art and an animated crowd. Face a computer opponent, rebound off the ropes, dive from the turnbuckles, or take the fight to ringside. Each wrestler has a signature move and a distinct throw.

## Match modes

- **Preset Match** lets you pick one of four prearranged bouts.
- **Custom Match** lets you select your wrestler, your opponent, and the arena.

The roster includes Ultimate Warrior, Honky Tonk Man, Bret “Hitman” Hart, Yokozuna, Diamond Dallas Page, “Macho Man” Randy Savage, Booker T, and The Undertaker. The four arenas are SummerSlam ’88, WrestleMania X, Spring Stampede ’97, and Judgment Day ’04.

Use the arrow keys to navigate the menus and press `Enter` or `Space` to confirm. Pressing `↑` from the top row moves focus to **Back**. The VS screen introduces both wrestlers before the match starts. Press `Esc` during the intro to return to the previous screen.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys or `WASD` | Move |
| `I` | Jump or dive from the turnbuckle |
| `E` | Climb or step down from a turnbuckle, or pass under the ropes |
| `J` | Strike on the mat or in the air |
| `Shift+J` | Use your signature move while standing in the ring |
| `K`, then `K` again | Grapple a standing opponent, then throw |
| `J → K → K` or `J → J → K → K` | Follow a landed strike with a combo and finish with a stronger throw |
| `L` | Block or grab the ropes to break a pin |
| `Space` | Start or maintain a pin, kick out, or escape a standing grapple |
| `T` | Taunt the crowd |
| `Esc` | Pause or resume the match |

## Match rules

- **Pins.** Press `Space` near a downed opponent to start a pin. Tap it to keep the opponent down or to kick out if you are being pinned. A three-count wins the match. An opponent with more stamina is harder to pin.
- **Countouts.** The referee counts to ten while a wrestler is outside the ring. The wrestler still outside at ten loses. If both wrestlers are outside, the match ends in a draw.
- **Time limit.** The clock runs for two minutes. A pin or grapple already in progress can finish after the clock runs out. If neither wrestler wins, the match ends in a draw.
- **Stamina.** Low stamina slows movement, weakens strikes, and makes standing grapples harder to escape. Tap `Space` before the throw begins.
- **Ropes and turnbuckles.** Move into the ropes and release the direction key to rebound. Strike during or just after the rebound for a stronger running hit. A turnbuckle dive knocks down an opponent if you land on them. Wait too long at the top and the opponent may try to shove you off.
- **Signature moves and combos.** Signature moves have a five-second cooldown. Press the next key in a combo within 0.65 seconds of landing a hit.
- **Taunts.** Playing to the crowd leaves your wrestler briefly open to attack.

Use **Settings → Sound** to toggle crowd audio and the VS intro sound. Your choice is saved between visits.
